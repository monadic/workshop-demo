
First, what does the chart really install?

    helm template renders the chart with these values, the way Argo CD does, 
    into work/bitnami.yaml. Nothing reaches a cluster.

$ helm template shop-redis oci://registry-1.docker.io/bitnamicharts/redis --version 25.5.3 --namespace shop -f values.yaml > work/bitnami.yaml
Pulled: registry-1.docker.io/bitnamicharts/redis:25.5.3
