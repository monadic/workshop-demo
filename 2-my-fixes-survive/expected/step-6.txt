    cub unit create stores a file as a Unit. This one is what the assistant 
    wrote two weeks ago, before you touched it. The assistant owns this Unit.

$ cub unit create --space workshop-demo-shop shop-web-generated app-generated.yaml
Successfully created unit shop-web-generated (166d8c91-49fe-4dba-a698-8042171f641a)

    This makes your own copy, cloned from the assistant's. ConfigHub keeps the 
    link between the two.

$ cub unit create --space workshop-demo-shop shop-web --upstream-unit shop-web-generated --upstream-space workshop-demo-shop
